# Human Review Adapter - Controlled Documentary Specification

This is a controlled documentary specification of the `human-review-adapter` in the YZI OS Research Lab. It is documentary only. It is pure Markdown, with no frontmatter, no YAML, no JSON, no pseudo-schema, and no machine-readable contract. It creates no executable adapter, no adapter code, no runner, no registry, no human review integration, and authorizes no execution.

## 1. Adapter Specification Title

`human-review-adapter`

## 2. Documentary Status

`DOCUMENTARY_SPECIFICATION_ONLY`

## 3. Purpose

This specification exists only to describe, at a documentary and conceptual level, how a future `human-review-adapter` could translate institutional capabilities, documentary evidence, boundaries, and stop conditions into a controlled human review step. It does not execute human review, does not call human reviewers automatically, does not start review workflows, does not approve decisions by itself, and does not convert human review into automatic approval.

## 4. Institutional Principle

`Spec is authority. Institutional skill is capability. Adapter is translation. Executor is replaceable.`
