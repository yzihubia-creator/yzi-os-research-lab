# Stop Conditions Specification Title

controlled-harness-governance-stop-conditions

# Documentary Status

DOCUMENTARY_HARNESS_GOVERNANCE_STOP_CONDITIONS_SPECIFICATION_ONLY

# Purpose

This document defines the controlled documentary governance stop conditions for future harnesses.

It exists only to describe when a future harness must stop, block advancement, or require human review before any continuation.

It does not create an executable harness, technical harness, runner, registry, detector, logger, validator, policy engine, checker, workflow, pipeline, code, API, database, schema, migration, persistence, technical log, command, subagent, adapter, or executor.

# Institutional Principle

Spec is authority. Institutional skill is capability. Adapter is translation. Executor is replaceable.

# Stop Conditions Scope

This specification defines only documentary stop conditions for future harnesses.

It does not create an executable stopping mechanism.

# Stop Conditions Nature

Stop conditions are documentary governance rules.

Stop conditions guide stopping, blocking, and human review.

Stop conditions are not code.

Stop conditions are not executable policy.

Stop conditions do not execute correction.

Stop conditions do not authorize the next task.

# Governance Violation Severity Guidance

LOW means textual inconsistency without technical creation and without execution.

MEDIUM means documentary divergence that may compromise evidence, readiness, or gate.

HIGH means creation or modification outside scope, critical corrupted output, or absence of gate.

CRITICAL means unauthorized execution, prohibited technical creation, alteration of an approved spec, creation of YAML, JSON, or machine-readable contract when prohibited, or automatic start of the next task.

This guidance is documentary only and is not a schema.

This guidance is not YAML.

This guidance is not JSON.

# Human Review Requirement

Every MEDIUM, HIGH, or CRITICAL stop condition requires human review before advancement.

A LOW stop condition may require controlled documentary correction, but still does not authorize the next task.

No stop condition can be corrected automatically by an executable harness in this phase.

Human review cannot be inferred.
