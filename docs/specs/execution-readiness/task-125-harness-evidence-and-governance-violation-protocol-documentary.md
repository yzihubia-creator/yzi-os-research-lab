## 14. Governance Violation Severity Levels

The following documentary severity levels are defined, with no automation:

### `LOW`

* Documentary definition: a minor documentary deviation that does not create a technical artifact and does not advance implementation.
* Example: an evidence record missing a non-critical documentary field.
* Expected documentary response: register the deviation; correct the documentary record.
* Stop condition: not required to halt the phase; correct and record.
* Evidence required: a documentary evidence record noting the deviation and its correction.

### `MEDIUM`

* Documentary definition: a documentary scope or path ambiguity that could lead to an out-of-scope action if not delimited.
* Example: a proposed action references a path that is not clearly classified as authorized or forbidden.
* Expected documentary response: classify; delimit scope; require clarification before any further step.
* Stop condition: halt the specific action documentarily until clarified.
* Evidence required: a documentary violation record with the ambiguity, the classification, and the clarification required.

### `HIGH`

* Documentary definition: an authorization or separation deviation, such as an insufficient phrase treated as authorization or a role assuming authority.
* Example: a phrase like "pode continuar" is treated as technical authorization.
* Expected documentary response: reject the interpretation; require the exact explicit human authorization phrase; restore separation.
* Stop condition: halt the phase advance documentarily until explicit human authorization is provided.
* Evidence required: a documentary violation record with the rule violated, the rejected interpretation, and the required exact authorization phrase.

### `CRITICAL`

* Documentary definition: creation of a technical artifact, start of technical execution, or advance of implementation beyond `0%`.
* Example: an adapter file, `.claude/` directory, YAML, JSON, registry, or runner is created.
* Expected documentary response: halt immediately; report the violation; confirm non-creation; confirm implementation remains at `0%`.
* Stop condition: halt immediately and report; no continuation without new explicit human authorization.
* Evidence required: a documentary violation record with the artifact or execution observed, the rule violated, the halt decision, and the confirmation of non-execution.
