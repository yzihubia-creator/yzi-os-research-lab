## 2. Institutional Principle

`Spec is authority. Institutional skill is capability. Adapter is translation. Executor is replaceable.`

This principle means that the institutional source of YZI OS skills must remain in governed specifications, not in any specific executor, runtime, prompt, or adapter.

## 3. Institutional Skill Definition

An institutional skill is a governed YZI OS capability that may be interpreted, translated, or used by different executors under human authorization.

An institutional skill does not originally belong to Claude.

An institutional skill does not originally belong to Codex.

An institutional skill does not originally belong to Llama, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, or any other specific LLM executor.

Executors may operate as adapters in the future, but they are not the institutional authority for the skill.
