# YZI OS Institutional Skills

## Purpose

This directory is the future institutional skills layer for YZI OS.

It exists as a documentary institutional source for future governed skills. It does not create operational skills, adapters, runners, commands, subagents, manifests, executable contracts, YAML, JSON, or code.

## Institutional Principle

`Spec is authority. Institutional skill is capability. Adapter is translation. Executor is replaceable.`

## Institutional Skill Definition

An institutional skill is a governed YZI OS capability derived from approved specs.

An institutional skill may be adapted in the future for multiple executors, but its institutional meaning must remain governed by approved specs and human authorization.

## Separation of Responsibilities

* Spec: source of authority.
* Institutional Skill: governed capability.
* Subagent: specialized executor role.
* Adapter: translation for a specific tool or executor.
* Executor: Claude Code, Codex, Llama, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, or another environment capable of executing tasks.

## Executor Independence

YZI OS institutional skills do not originally belong to Claude, Codex, Llama, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, or any other LLM.

Executors are replaceable. Institutional capability remains governed by YZI OS specs.

## Relationship With Adapters

Future adapters may translate institutional skills for specific execution environments.

Adapters are not institutional authority. Adapters must not alter institutional intent, redefine governance, or convert documentary acceptance into technical execution.
